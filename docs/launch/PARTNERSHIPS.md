# Partnership targets: Mumbai MMR and India

Prepared 26 Nov 2026 (IST). **Status of every row: not contacted, nothing confirmed.** Facts marked with a source come from web search on 26 Nov 2026; everything else is marked "to verify". Contact names, emails and current programs must be found and confirmed before outreach (use official "partner", "contact" or "advertise" pages; do not use scraped personal data).
