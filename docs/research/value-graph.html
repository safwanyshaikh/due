Due Value Graph: make every rupee of subscription pay back at least twice

The moat is not a list of offers. It is a per-user graph that knows what you pay, how you actually live (rides, orders, recharges, metro trips), and what each plan really returns, with the source and expiry of every benefit. The engine then shows the one move that saves the most, and proves the saving afterwards from real receipts.

Brutal truth: “we save you double” cannot be promised from offer pages alone. It becomes a real, defensible claim only when Due measures it per user from receipts. So the core KPI is the Return Ratio = verified savings ÷ Due fee, with a written guarantee once data proves it.

Worked example from your screenshot: is Uber One worth ₹149 for you?

Screenshot facts: ₹149/month, first month free, “Get 10% off 1 year”, up to 10% Uber One credits on Cars/Auto/Bike, Zomato Gold 3 months complimentary*, Uber’s claim “members save ₹210 on average”. Rules from Uber India’s published terms (as reported): credits on the final fare excluding taxes and tolls, capped at ₹150 a trip, credits expire after 60 days, no credits on the part paid with credits. This is exactly the calculation Due runs for every membership.

City and country packs

Every market is a config pack, not code: currency and tax, operator list per city (ride-hailing, bike taxi, auto, metro lines, suburban rail, city bus, premium shuttle), fare and pass tables, membership catalog, local laws, and the evidence sources. New city = new pack plus a research sprint.

  • Mumbai MMR first: Uber, Ola, Rapido, Cityflo, BEST via Chalo, Metro 1, 2A/7, 3, Navi Mumbai Metro, NMMT, TMT, Mumbai One app.
  • Next India: Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad (Namma Yatri, ONDC metro tickets).
  • Abroad later: UAE (Careem Plus moved from Dh19 to Dh29 a month in July 2026), Southeast Asia (GrabUnlimited), US (Lyft Go replaced Lyft Pink from 5 Nov 2026; Uber One).

How Due learns local habits without spying

  • Receipts the user forwards or connects (Uber, Rapido, Cityflo, Chalo, Swiggy, Zomato, recharge emails) give trips, fares and frequency.
  • Card statements and, later, Account Aggregator data via a licensed FIU partner give spend by merchant.
  • One shared SMS or a screenshot like yours becomes a reviewed observation, never a silent scrape.
  • Home and office area is asked once, as a choice of locality, to know which operators serve the user.

Questions only you can answer

  1. “Jio jameen” — what exactly did you mean? Search found Jio’s 2026 bundles (JioHotstar, 50 GB JioAICloud, 18-month Gemini Pro, and “1% extra on Jio Gold” via Jio Finance). Is it Jio Gold, a land or property offer, or something else? Send a screenshot.
  2. The 2× promise: a marketing target, or a written guarantee (for example, “if verified savings are below 2× your Due fee in a year, the next year is free”)? A guarantee is the stronger moat but needs legal wording and 3–6 months of real data first.
  3. Money from partners: may Due ever earn referral or affiliate fees (for example from Uber One sign-ups)? If yes, recommendations must still rank by user value only, with the fee disclosed. If no, it is a cleaner trust story.
  4. Order of cities after Mumbai MMR, and the first country abroad.
  5. Bank data: are you willing to go through an Account Aggregator FIU partner (cost and contract) in phase 3, or stay receipts-only?
  6. Screenshots: may Due ask users to share in-app screens like this Uber One one, to learn personalised offers that never appear on public pages?
