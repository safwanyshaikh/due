EMI switch check: is moving your loan to another bank worth it?

  • How a balance transfer works: new bank pays off the old loan → you pay the new EMI → old bank issues closure letter and returns papers.
  • What decides approval: credit score (lenders quote 700–750+), income, repayment history, property value for home loans.
  • Hidden costs: processing fee (about 0.5% to several %), legal and valuation fees, stamp duty, hard credit enquiries.
  • Exit cost: floating-rate individual loans have no prepayment charge (from 1 Jan 2026); fixed-rate personal loans and card EMIs often still charge 2–4%.
  • Card debt: card interest commonly 30–45% a year; card balance-transfer EMIs are advertised at about 11–16%.
  • Data Due needs: outstanding, rate, EMI, months left, rate type; from loan statement upload now, from credit bureau or Account Aggregator through a licensed partner later.
