## 2. What Due is (and is not)
Due helps you see and plan your bills, subscriptions, EMIs, card dues and renewals. It is **information only**. ## 4. Consent, one purpose at a time
Each optional purpose (sync/backup, email forwarding, Gmail, email reminders, household sharing, analytics) has its own switch and its own consent record with a timestamp and notice version. Not agreeing to an optional purpose does not stop you using the rest of Due. **Withdraw any consent at any time** in Settings → Account (or by writing to us). Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent. After withdrawal we stop that processing and delete the related data unless you ask us to keep it or the law requires it.

## 5. Gmail and Google user data — Limited Use disclosure
Due's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

- **Scopes:** `gmail.readonly` (restricted), `openid`, and `userinfo.email`. We never request send, modify, compose, delete or settings scopes.
- **What we use it for:** only to provide the user-facing feature you turned on: finding billing/renewal/trial/EMI/invoice messages and showing extracted fields for you to review. We search only for those message types.
- **No ads:** we do not use Google user data for serving ads, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based advertising.
- **No sale or transfer:** we do not sell Google user data. We do not transfer it to others except (a) as needed to provide or improve the user-facing feature, with our subprocessors listed in SUBPROCESSORS.md bound to confidentiality, (b) to comply with applicable law, or (c) as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice to you.
- **No human reading:** our staff do not read your Gmail content unless you give explicit consent for specific messages (for example, for support), it is necessary for security (for example, investigating abuse), or the law requires it.
- **No AI model training:** we do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI/ML models.
- **Storage:** tokens are encrypted. We do not keep message bodies by default. If an excerpt is kept it is redacted, encrypted and deleted after 3 days. Extracted fields stay until you delete them.
- **Disconnect:** disconnect in Settings → Gmail at any time. We revoke the token with Google; you can also revoke at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your account revokes all Gmail grants.
