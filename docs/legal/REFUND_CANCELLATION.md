## 1. Free beta
While Due is in free beta nothing is charged, so there is nothing to refund. This policy applies once paid plans ("Plus") start.

## 2. Cancel any time
- Cancel in the app: **Account → Plan → Cancel subscription**. No call or email needed. You can also email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account email and we will cancel for you.
- Cancelling stops all future charges at the payment gateway (Cashfree or Razorpay) straight away.
- **Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for**, then your account returns to the free plan. Your data is kept (subject to free-plan limits) unless you delete it.
- If a UPI AutoPay or card mandate was set up, it is cancelled with the subscription. You can also revoke it in your UPI/bank app.

## 3. Refunds
Subscription fees for a period already started are generally **not refundable**, because the digital service is available immediately. We will refund in these cases:
| Case | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Duplicate charge for the same period | Full amount of the extra charge |
| Charge taken but Plus not activated (failed or stuck payment) | Full amount, unless Plus is activated within [48 hours] |
| Charge after you cancelled (and before period end) because of our error | Full amount of that charge |
| First-time paid subscriber asks within [7] days and has not [used paid features: confirm] | [Optional goodwill refund: business to decide] |
| Technical fault that stops you using Plus for a long period and we cannot fix it | Pro-rata refund at our discretion |
| Unauthorised transaction | Report within [3] days; handled under the gateway and RBI rules |

## 4. How to ask
Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with your account email, date, amount and the gateway payment/transaction id. We reply within [2] working days.

## 5. Timelines
- We aim to approve or reject a refund request within [5 working days].
- Approved refunds go back to the **original payment method** only. Gateways usually take **5 to 7 working days** after we initiate it (up to [10] working days for some banks/cards). UPI is often faster.
- For failed payments where money was debited but not received by us, the gateway or bank usually auto-reverses within the timelines set by RBI (generally T+5 working days). If not, write to us.

## 6. Taxes
GST collected is refunded with the amount refunded. Credit notes are issued as the law requires.

## 7. Not refundable
Fees already used; charges from your own providers, bank or UPI app (Due does not pay anything for you); and periods after you were told a charge would renew and did not cancel in time, except as in section 3.
