## 1. Delete inside the app
1. Sign in (a sign-in within the last 15 minutes is needed; the app will ask again).
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account** and confirm.
3. If you have a paid plan, the subscription is cancelled at the gateway first.

## 2. Delete from the web (without the app)
- Open https://due.karigarai.com, sign in with your email link, and use **Account → Delete account**; or
- Email [GRIEVANCE EMAIL] from your account email with the subject "Delete my Due account". We will verify you (by a link to that email) and delete. If you cannot sign in, tell us what you can confirm; we may ask for more proof.

## 3. What happens and when
| Step | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in sessions end and the account is disabled | Immediately |
| Data is removed in steps: sessions, households, forwarding addresses, forwarded messages, intake items, Gmail connections and imported data, reminders, notifications, sign-in links, invites, audit trail, account record | Within [7] days (normally within hours); progress shown at the deletion-status page |
| Gmail access is revoked at Google | At the start of deletion |
| Backups | Overwritten within 30 days |
| Confirmation | Email/status page |

## 4. What we keep after deletion
- Payment and invoice records (gateway subscription ids, plan, amounts, dates): up to 8 years where GST/Companies Act law requires; unlinked from your email and account.
- A deletion tombstone (account id and date, no personal data) to prove deletion.
- Security logs needed under CERT-In Directions: up to 365 days, without document or mail content.
- Anything we must keep for a legal claim or court/authority order.

## 5. Delete only some data
- Delete single items, raw forwarded messages, the forwarding address, or imported Gmail items in the app.
- Disconnect Gmail (Settings → Gmail), with an option to delete imported items.
- Turn off sync and delete synced copies; data on your device remains until you clear it.
- Withdraw any consent (Settings → Account → Privacy choices).
- Data only on your device (statements and files read on the device) is deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling.

## 6. Export first
Settings → Account → Export gives you a copy of your data.

## 7. Help

## 8. Unused accounts
If you do not sign in or sync for 24 months (an admin-set period, never less than 12 months), we email you 30 days and again 7 days before deleting the account, using the same process as a deletion you ask for. Signing in once, or syncing, keeps your account. We do not delete an account that has an active paid plan, and we do not delete if we cannot email you a warning.
